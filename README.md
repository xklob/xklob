## Featured: [YouCanSeeEverything](https://github.com/xklob/YouCanSeeEverything)

**An omni-dashboard for your digital history.**

Bring personal data exports from different services into one searchable local archive. Browse records and attached media, revisit individual days, explore locations and trips, and open the original source files.

- Search across imported sources, with visible coverage and gaps.
- Ask questions and generate day or month summaries with citations to selected records.
- Retain originals by content hash, deduplicate identical files, and resume interrupted indexing.

Built with **Python, SQLite, and React**. Personal exports and configuration stay outside the public repository. Optional AI features send selected excerpts to the model.
